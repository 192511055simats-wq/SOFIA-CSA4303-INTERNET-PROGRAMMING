GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Demo policy: check-in from 2:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM.

Can I change or cancel a reservation?

For this demonstration, guests can modify or cancel a confirmed booking at least 3 days before check-in.

Does the website process real payments?

No. Payment selection is simulated for a frontend practical. Never enter real card or bank details.

How do I see my bookings?

Log in and open My dashboard to see reservations and their current status.
