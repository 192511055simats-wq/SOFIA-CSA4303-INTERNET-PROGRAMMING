How do I book a bus ticket? +

Choose your source, destination, travel date, and passenger count. Select a bus, choose seats, enter passenger details, and finish the demo checkout.

Can I cancel a booking? +

Open My Bookings from your dashboard and select Cancel. This changes the local demo record; real operator cancellation rules apply in a live service.

Does this site take real payments? +

No. This is a frontend lab demonstration. Checkout is simulated and does not process real payments or send actual emails.
